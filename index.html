The Evolution of Photography

A journey through the remarkable history of cameras

Scroll to explore
1021

Camera Obscura Invented

The Camera Obscura was originally used to view Solar eclipses and began to be used as an aid for drawing. This optical device laid the foundation for modern photography.

1685

First Portable Camera

The first portable camera was developed, making it easier for artists and scientists to capture images on the go.

1826

World's First Photograph

Joseph Nicéphore Niépce achieved the first photographic image using a camera obscura. The exposure took approximately 8 hours.

1837-1839

Daguerreotype Camera

Louis Daguerre unveiled the daguerreotype on January 7, 1839. This was the first commercially successful photographic process, invented by Louis-Jacques-Mandé Daguerre and Joseph Nicéphore Niépce.

1851

Wet Emulsion Plates

Frederick Scott Archer invented the Collodion process, which reduced light exposure time to just 2-3 seconds, revolutionizing photography.

1888

Kodak's First Commercial Camera

George Eastman introduced roll film in 1885 and launched the Kodak camera in 1888. The Brownie camera went on sale, making photography accessible to amateurs and professionals alike.

1900

The Brownie Camera

Kodak sells the first mass-marketed camera called the Brownie, making photography available to the general public at an affordable price.

1925

First 35mm Still Camera

Leica introduced the first 35mm still camera. This format quickly gained popularity due to its compact size and high image quality.

1935

Kodachrome Color Film

Color photos created using subtractive color process, known as Kodachrome film, revolutionizing color photography.

1947-1948

Polaroid Instant Camera

Edwin Land launches the Polaroid camera, introducing instant image development. This innovation changed photography forever.

1973-1978

First Digital Camera

Steven Sasson invents the first digital camera and receives U.S. patent, marking the beginning of the digital photography era.

1986

First Disposable Camera

Fujifilm produced the first disposable camera, making photography even more accessible and convenient.

1991

First Professional Digital Camera

Kodak released the first professional digital camera system (DCS) which was of a great use for photojournalists. It was a modified Nikon F-3 camera with a 1.3 megapixel sensor.

2000

First Camera Phone

The first cell phone with a camera is produced in Japan, marking the beginning of smartphone photography.

2016

AI-Powered Photography

Drone photography improves with DJI's Phantom 4 which uses computer vision and machine learning to track and photograph objects rather than following GPS signal.
